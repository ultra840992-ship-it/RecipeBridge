## 2. DESIGN AESTHETICS & VISUAL DIRECTIVES (Stitch System Specs)

### 🎨 Color Palette (Cyber Amber & Dark Glassmorphism)
- Primary Canvas: Deep Midnight Obsidian (`#0f172a` / `#090d16`)
- Card Surfaces: Semi-transparent Frosted Glass (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)
- Primary Accent: Glowing Amber Gold (`#f59e0b` / `#d4af37`) — Signals value, achievement, and premium career verification
- Secondary Accent: Cyber Emerald Green (`#10b981`) — Smart Contract status, active matching, verified badges
- Tertiary Accent: Electric Violet (`#8b5cf6`) — AI Prompt Recipe Builder & AI tool combinations
- Divider & Border: Thin Neon Hairlines (`0.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`)
- Typography Colors: Pure White (`#ffffff`), Muted Slate (`#94a3b8`), Golden Amber (`#fbbf24`)

### 📐 Typography & Geometry
- Font Family: Modern Geometric Sans (Inter / Outfit / Roboto / Plus Jakarta Sans)
- Heading Hierarchy: Bold 32px ~ 40px Display Titles with subtle gradient text (`linear-gradient(to right, #ffffff, #f59e0b)`)
- Card Edges: Sleek Rounded Corners (`12px ~ 16px border-radius`)
- Elevation & Depth: Multi-layered glass panels with subtle outer glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.37)`)

---

## 3. CORE PLATFORM MODULES TO GENERATE (4 Essential Screens)

### Module 01: Micro-Project Job Board (과제 탐색 & 매칭)

- Metadata: Creator handle, download counts, price tags (e.g., "₩45,000"), and star ratings.
